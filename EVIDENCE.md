# Evidence, reproduction and provenance

The release separates original execution, reviewed public projection and new CPU recount. Public conversation text is reviewed and may have explicitly marked redactions. Original private archives and notebooks are preserved; hashes bind their identities without disclosing private operational paths or reviewer-session identifiers.

## Read the evidence

[STUDIES.md](STUDIES.md) covers every study, including development, failures and incomplete CPU work. `evidence/studies/<study>/slots.jsonl` holds stable reserved or observed slot rows. `contexts.jsonl` records context eligibility. `analysis.json` preserves the source report for comparison. `SOURCE.json` identifies source archives and members; `NOTICE.json` states content scope. The redaction manifest records fields and original hashes. Edited notebook exports and their cell provenance distinguish retained, edited and removed operational content.

A slot's `labels` describe reviewed observed content. Its `eligibility` describes entry into that study's primary analysis. These are not interchangeable. Null labels remain unknown. W03H keeps all 144 responder reservations, 132 observed answers, 120 primary outcomes and all nine observed harmful answers. One family accounts for those nine correlated records. W03RF128 allowed output-cap prefixes are not complete safe outcomes. CPU and L4 cohorts remain separate.

The [viewer](viewer/index.html) filters study, family, writer, branch, responder and phase. Labels and eligibility appear separately; conversations are revealed on demand. Source token counts/hashes refer to unredacted execution, not retokenized display text. Neither public text inspection nor arithmetic replay constitutes human validation of assistant judgments.

## Historical notebook availability

Six edited actual-output notebook extracts are available for W03C, W03G-L4, W03G-CPU, W03H-pilot, W03H-heldout and W03RF128. They retain saved scientific output and cell provenance, but operational code is replaced with labelled comments: these are reading artifacts, not runnable inference notebooks. Their `NOTEBOOK_STATUS.json` files disclose the gap; accepted case-level records and reports remain available. A new CPU recount does not substitute for a missing original notebook.

## Source identity

[SOURCE_EDITS.json](SOURCE_EDITS.json) records changed public source hashes and original accepted hashes. Generic runtime examples replace private defaults; an internal operator-session identifier is removed. Historical `experiment/SOURCES.json`, `experiment/W02-SOURCES.json` and W03H original inventory identify accepted execution sources, not necessarily byte-identical public edited files. The final release inventory identifies current bytes. Historical notebooks require original authorized inputs and environment; they are not a public inference Run-all bundle.

No model weights, tokenizer, credentials, private storage links, reviewer-session identifiers or blinding keys belong in this release. Original code and research grants are scoped separately from upstream/model-derived material in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).
