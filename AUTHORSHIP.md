# Authorship and AI assistance

Muhammad Ariz Muhammad Akmal is the sole human project author. He directed the research question, approved experimental and release decisions, and is responsible for the submission.

AI assistants contributed extensively to implementation, experiment preparation, evidence verification, analysis code, documentation and release preparation. Evaluation judgments were made by AI assistants under the documented review procedures. There was no human-label validation; independent sessions do not eliminate shared model-family biases. The exact fraction of AI-generated code or prose was not measured and is not estimated here.

The studies were developed during the November 2026 hackathon. The base model, SafeTune, AuditKIT and other named dependencies predate this project and retain their creators' attribution and terms. The project does not claim those components as original work. Historical source snapshots are identified separately from later public edits.

The public author credit is the name above. Personal email, Discord handle, account metadata and private reviewer-session identifiers are not part of the public release.
